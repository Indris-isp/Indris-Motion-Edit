# SignWell — 30-second launch video

**Company:** SignWell is e-signature software for small businesses: upload a document, place the fields, send it, and the client signs on any device. It takes printing, scanning and chasing signatures off their plate.

**Audience:** owners, freelancers, and sales/ops leads at 1–50 person businesses (agencies, consultants, landlords, contractors).

**Goal:** launch video for the website hero and paid social.

**One message:** send it, they sign it, it's done. No more paper chase.

**Must show:**
1. The hook: a contract stuck waiting, follow-up emails piling up.
2. The problem: the print → sign → scan → email loop, and the days it costs.
3. The product: placing a signature field and hitting Send in the SignWell UI.
4. The payoff: the client signs on their phone and the status flips to Signed. Days become minutes.
5. Logo + CTA.

**Tone:** confident, clean, fast. Premium SaaS launch, not cartoonish. No voiceover, music + SFX only.

**Deliverables:** 30 s master in 16:9 (1920 × 1080, 60 fps). Cutdowns dropped from rev 3 on.

**Assets (still needed from SignWell):** Figma file with the UI screens, brand guide (colors, fonts), logo SVG, music license.
Until they arrive, the UI is drawn as vector placeholders, colors and fonts live in `src/config.ts`, and the soundtrack is a synthesized temp track.

**Deadline:** TBC, before launch.
