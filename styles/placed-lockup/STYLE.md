# Placed Lockup: caption style guide (reference 2)

Reference: "Dreamy Brunch Ideas" reel (Klickpin upload), 720×1280, 30 fps, 35.3 s, 1,060 frames. Every frame was extracted; contact sheets at 5 fps with frame numbers are in `reference/contact_5fps_*.jpg`, full-resolution crops of each compositing effect in `reference/crops*.jpg`.

**The idea in one line:** every caption is *designed into its shot*. It is placed in that shot's negative space or on one of its surfaces, built word by word as a small-plus-big lockup, and blended so it looks like part of the picture.

## 3. Where the captions go, and why

1. **Negative space first.** Text sits where the shot is empty: sky, wall, floor, horizon, the band above a subject. It never covers a face.
2. **Words take the place of what they describe:** "lonely." sits in the empty desert, "changing" behind a man who doesn't move, "inspiration" on the light, "different" on the page being written.
3. **Follow the lines of the shot.** Text rotates with a stair rail or a page and curves around a face, so it reads as part of the scene instead of a layer on top.
4. **Depth.** Big words often pass behind the subject's head or hair, fully hidden there with no ghosting. This only happens where the hidden letters can be guessed.
5. **Lockup hierarchy.** Connector words are small (about 0.35–0.4× size), and the meaning word is big. The small line hugs the big word's left or right edge, above or below it.
6. **Rhythm.** A new shot comes with each phrase, and words build within the shot as they're spoken. The fast cut section (the drums) drops to single small words.

## 4. Composite modes

| Mode | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Normal, white | default: dark or mid backgrounds | plain white type |
| **Difference** | bright areas: blonde hair, lamp, white paper | text inverts against whatever is behind it, so one word can be white in one part and coloured or dark in another |
| **Overlay** | surfaces: the poster | the surface's texture shows inside the letters, so they look printed |
| Opacity ~75% | the sky | the sky colour bleeds through, softer than solid white |
| Occlusion (matte) | text behind heads or hair | the subject sits fully in front; no ghosting |

## 5. Animation

- Small words cut in on their spoken word with no transition.
- Big words fade in over about 6 frames (0.2 s).
- Lines build downward. Earlier lines never move when a new one is added.
- Occasionally the small lead line dims as the big word lands ("slightly less" → "painful").
- A big word can scale up to track a camera push ("fun").
- The opening is a letterbox reveal. The CTA is stacked capitals built word by word.

## 6. Type

- Bold grotesk (Inter Display here; the reference looks like SF Pro or Helvetica Now).
- Big words: ExtraBold, tight tracking, sized so the word spans about 50–60% of the frame width.
- Small words: SemiBold.
- Titles and calls to action: Black, in capitals.
- Mostly lowercase, and the speech's punctuation is kept.

## 7. How it was applied to "Passion Finds You" (v11)

The source is one speaker in four setups, with no B-roll, so the rules above are applied to what each setup offers:

| Shot | Where the text goes | Mode |
|---|---|---|
| Wide stage, 0–2.4 s | on the red screen above her: "everything I was / **searching** / for", "was searching / **for me,**" | **Overlay** (lifted 30% toward white so it stays legible): the screen's texture shows through, like the reference's poster |
| White slide, 2.4–9.9 s | across her body, so each line crosses both her dark blazer and the white slide | **Difference**: the same word is black over the slide and light over the blazer, like the reference's "in the beginning." |
| Dark wall, 9.9–16.5 s | over her chest (like "fun" on the boxer); "**all along,**" curved down the right side of her face (like "around you") | Normal |
| Red slide, 16.5–26.4 s | lower third over her jacket; thesis as stacked caps "CLARITY / COMES THROUGH / ACTION."; "**how to swim,**" lockup at chest; closing call to action "JUMP / INTO THE / WATER." in caps, like "START / TODAY" | Normal |

Placement is computed from her matte: head position, the free side, and torso centre over each lockup's exact duration. Files: `projects/passion-finds-you/build/placed_style/spec.json` (words, roles, zones, blends) and `elements.json` (final positions and timings). Tools: `spec2.py`, `layout2.py`, `lines.cjs`, `arc.cjs`, `composite2.py`.

Not carried over:
- **Letterbox reveal:** you asked earlier for it to be removed.
- **Cutting to new footage per phrase:** there's only one camera subject, so there's nothing to cut to.
