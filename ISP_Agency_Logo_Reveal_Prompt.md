## Main Prompt

```
Minimal flat 2D motion-graphics logo animation, vertical 9:16, solid warm off-white background (#F3EFE6), no texture, no gradients. Clean Swiss design, crisp vector shapes, smooth ease-in-out motion with slight bounce.

0–2s: A single small black dot drops into the center of the frame and bounces once. It splits into three dots in a horizontal row, then the middle dot slides out, leaving two.

2–5s: The left dot morphs into a thick black L-shaped corner bracket, drawn on stroke by stroke, opening toward the bottom-right. A small, thin sans-serif label fades in under it: "Structure".

5–8s: Next to it, a burnt-orange circle (#D9621F) grows from a tiny point into a solid disc with a soft pop. A label appears under it: "Focus". Then a second black L-shaped bracket draws on to the right, mirrored and opening toward the top-left, with the label "Clarity".

8–9.5s: The labels fade out. The three shapes move together and rotate into a diagonal arrangement: the top-left bracket, the orange circle in the middle, and the bottom-right bracket. Together they form an open viewfinder frame around the orange circle, with the brackets not touching.

9.5–10.5s: Hard cut to a solid black background. The brackets turn off-white and the orange circle stays orange. The shapes do a quick snap, settling and reshuffling into their final positions.

10.5–12s: Hard cut back to the off-white background. The final logo mark sits on the left. The wordmark "ISP Agency" slides in from the right: "ISP" in heavy bold geometric sans and "Agency" in regular weight, both near-black. Below it, a tiny letter-spaced line fades in: "CREATIVE EDITING STUDIO". Hold on the final frame.
```

## Production Tips

- **AI tools struggle with precise shapes and text.** For a clean result, generate the shape animation (0–9.5s) with AI using your logo image as the **end frame**, then add labels and the wordmark in CapCut or After Effects.
- **Pixel-perfect option:** build it in After Effects — it's only ~6 shape layers.
- **Sound design:** soft click on each shape draw, a "pop" on the orange core, a whoosh on the black cut, a bass hit when the wordmark lands.
- **Customize:** "Structure / Focus / Clarity" and "CREATIVE EDITING STUDIO" are placeholders — swap in your own words and tagline.
