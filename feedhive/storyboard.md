**Scene 1 · 180 posts (hook)** — 0:00–0:03, frames 0–180
- On screen: dark background, red glow. A phone buzzes; "Post due today" banners stack up and spill over its top. Six channel badges slam in. Counter "Posts due this month 0 → 180". Headline: "180 posts. This month."
- Motion: f 0–24 banners stack; f 18–48 badges slam in from off-screen (3 f offset, overshoot, speed streaks); f 40–100 counter ease-out with tick SFX; f 96–180 headline word by word, holds 84 f.
- Camera: whip-in 125% → 100% in 14 f, then slow push 100 → 106%.

**Scene 2 · Tab chaos (problem)** — 0:03–0:07, frames 180–420
- On screen: seven tool windows (Notes, Canva, LinkedIn, Facebook, Spreadsheet, Instagram, X) fan out like a hand of cards. Cursor copies the caption and pastes it window to window. X counter goes red "312 / 280 ✕"; Instagram shows "⚠ Needs 4:5". Headline "7 tabs. 1 caption." Sub-line "Copy. Paste. Rewrite. Resize. Repeat."
- Motion: f 180–210 fan-out (3 f offset, ±12°); f 210–300 cursor arcs with click presses; f 300–330 red counter + shake, crop warning; f 330–420 sub-line word by word, windows jitter and never settle.
- Camera: handheld micro-shake ±3 px, easy-ease pan following the cursor (≥0.8 s per move).

**Scene 3 · Burnout (problem)** — 0:07–0:10, frames 420–600
- On screen: clock spins 9:00 AM → 11:47 PM. Week calendar with days flipping to red "✕ missed". Reach line drawing downward. Headline "…and still, gaps."
- Motion: f 420–480 clock spin with motion blur; f 470–530 missed days, 4 f stagger; f 520–580 reach line draws down; f 580–600 colour drains to 60% saturation (low point).
- Camera: slow push 100 → 108%, 1.5° dutch tilt.

**Scene 4 · The turn (transition)** — 0:10–0:11.5, frames 600–690
- On screen: every window, badge and the clock implodes into one point; blue flash and light rays; night flips to white; FeedHive mark. "Meet FeedHive."
- Motion: f 600–618 implode (ease-in, streak trails); f 618–630 flash + wipe (whoosh + hit SFX); f 630–690 mark pops with overshoot, line slides in 4 f later.
- Camera: fast push-in during the implosion, settles on the mark with overshoot.

**Scene 5 · Create with AI (solution)** — 0:11.5–0:17, frames 690–1020
- On screen: real FeedHive UI (Figma SVG): AI Assistant chat. Prompt "Create a post about latest trends in AI and business" → post streams in → "Let's include a few bullet points" → tracked changes red → green → Accept. The post then fans out into six channel cards, each with a green check ("280 ✓", "4:5 ✓", "9:16 ✓"…), fixing scene 2's red errors.
- Motion: f 690–790 typing + Send press, reply streams line by line; f 790–880 diff staggers, Accept press + glow; f 880–1020 fan-out, 3 f offset.
- Camera: zoom 130% on the prompt bar → ease back to 100% → pan right to the fan-out.

**Scene 6 · Automate (solution)** — 0:17–0:22, frames 1020–1320
- On screen: channel cards fly into the Smart Scheduling week at best times ("Tue 9:15"); the week fills. Automation flow: "New post published → Post Condition: performs well → Recycle in 30 days", with a loop arrow. Headline "Automate the rest."
- Motion: f 1020–1090 cards fly on arcs with trails, overshoot landing; f 1080–1200 slots fill, 2–4 f stagger; f 1180–1320 nodes connect, loop arrow spins once.
- Camera: easy-ease pan calendar → automation, overlapping the last 20% of the fill.

**Scene 7 · Before / After (payoff)** — 0:22–0:26, frames 1320–1560
- On screen: split screen. Left "BEFORE": desaturated Act 1 chaos, "180 due". Right "WITH FEEDHIVE": the full month with a green check on every day, "0 due ✓ — A month of content. Done."
- Motion: f 1320–1360 blue wipe left → right; f 1350–1420 checks ripple diagonally; f 1400–1460 counter 180 → 0 over 60 f; hold ≥ 90 f with 1 → 1.02 drift.
- Camera: pull back 115 → 100% to reveal both halves, then micro-push.

**Scene 8 · Logo + CTA (logo)** — 0:26–0:30, frames 1560–1800
- On screen: calendar folds into the mark, rings pulse. FeedHive wordmark, tagline "Social media using Automation & AI Agents.", CTA "Get started for free →", "feedhive.com · Double your AI credits for 3 months" (offer line optional).
- Motion: f 1560–1600 fold + rings; f 1596–1660 wordmark +4 f, tagline line by line; f 1660–1800 CTA pops + one press, slow drift to end.
- Camera: gentle push-in 100 → 104%.
