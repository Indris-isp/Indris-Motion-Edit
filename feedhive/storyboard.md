**Scene 1 · Six channels (hook)** — 0:00–0:03.5, frames 0–210
- On screen: white canvas with FeedHive's faint grid. One empty post card in the center with a blinking caret. Around it, six channel badges pop in (Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Facebook, TikTok, Threads — simple glyphs), each with a small red "due today" dot. Counter top-right: "Posts due this month 0 → 180". Kinetic headline under the card: "6 channels. Every day."
- Motion: badges pop in with overshoot, 3-frame offset each, then keep a slow ±4 px orbit drift. Counter counts up over 60 frames, ease-out. Headline word by word.
- Camera: slow push-in 100% → 106%.

**Scene 2 · The rewrite grind (problem)** — 0:03.5–0:09, frames 210–540
- On screen: three windows side by side: a notes doc, a LinkedIn-style composer, an X-style composer. The cursor copies a caption from the doc, arcs over and pastes it into LinkedIn, then pastes into X: the character counter turns red ("312 / 280"), the cursor deletes and rewrites. A small schedule picker opens: "11:47 PM". Text: "Write. Rewrite. Resize. Repeat."
- Motion: cursor on arcs, click presses (1 → 0.96 → 1) on copy, paste and the time picker. Red counter shakes once. Text words land on the beats of each action.
- Camera: easy-ease pan left → right following the cursor (≥0.8 s per window move), slight tilt-free micro-push throughout.

**Scene 3 · Create with AI (fix)** — 0:09–0:16, frames 540–960
- On screen: the windows collapse into the FeedHive workspace (Figma SVG, matching the site hero): AI Assistant chat on the left, post editor on the right. The FeedHive avatar says "Hi there 👋 I'm the FeedHive AI Assistant." Cursor types "Create a post about latest trends in AI and business" and clicks Send. The AI replies; the editor fills with the post, then a second ask ("Let's include a few bullet points") shows tracked changes: red strikethrough lines out, green bullet lines in. Cursor clicks **Accept**. Text: "Create with AI."
- Motion: Send and Accept button presses. Chat bubbles enter staggered (3 frames). Editor lines write in line by line; the diff lines stagger red → green.
- Camera: zoom to the prompt bar on typing (≈130%), ease back to 100% as the editor fills, gentle push toward the Accept button.

**Scene 4 · Automate (fix)** — 0:16–0:21.5, frames 960–1290
- On screen: the accepted post shrinks into a post card and flies into a weekly calendar (Smart Scheduling). The card duplicates into each channel's version and the week fills slot by slot with best-time chips ("Tue 9:15", "Thu 12:30"…). On the right, an automation flow builds: "New post → Post Condition: posted on Instagram → Recycle in 30 days". Text: "Automate."
- Motion: card fly on an arc with overshoot landing. Calendar slots fill with a 2–4 frame stagger, never all at once. Automation nodes connect with drawn lines (ease-in-out).
- Camera: easy-ease pan from the calendar to the automation flow, overlapping the end of the slot fill.

**Scene 5 · Content machine (payoff)** — 0:21.5–0:25.5, frames 1290–1530
- On screen: pull back to a full month view, every day filled, each with a small green check. The Scene 1 counter returns: "Posts due 180 → 0", replaced by "Scheduled ✓". A row of two avatars (a teammate and the FeedHive AI) with "Collaborate." chip. Text: "A month of content. Done."
- Motion: checks ripple across the calendar diagonally (staggered). Counter counts down over 60 frames. Hold the clear, full calendar ≥ 90 frames with a 1 → 1.02 drift.
- Camera: pull back 115% → 100%, then micro-push.

**Scene 6 · Logo + CTA (logo)** — 0:25.5–0:30, frames 1530–1800
- On screen: calendar scales away. FeedHive logo builds (the three-dot mark draws in, then the wordmark). Tagline: "Social media using Automation & AI Agents." CTA button: "Get started for free" with "feedhive.com" beneath. Small line: "Works with ChatGPT · Claude · MCP & API".
- Motion: logo mark pops with overshoot, wordmark slides from behind it 4 frames later, tagline offset by line, CTA button presses once. Slow drift to the end.
- Camera: gentle push-in 100% → 104%.
