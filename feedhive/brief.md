# FeedHive — 30-second launch video

**Company:** FeedHive is an AI social media management tool for creating, scheduling and automating posts across channels, with AI agents working alongside the team.

**Audience:** social media managers, marketers, creators and small marketing teams who post on several channels every day.

**Goal:** launch video for the website hero, Product Hunt and paid social (launch offer: "Double your AI credits for 3 months").

**One message:** stop juggling tabs and rewriting the same post six times. FeedHive's AI creates, schedules and automates it for you.

**Must show:**
1. The hook: one person, six channels, a post due on every one of them, every day.
2. The problem: write, rewrite per network, resize, schedule at midnight, by hand.
3. The product: the AI Assistant writing and editing a post in the real FeedHive UI (chat + editor with tracked changes, Accept), then Smart Scheduling and Automation filling the calendar.
4. The payoff: a full content calendar on autopilot.
5. Logo + CTA: "Get started for free · feedhive.com".

**Tone:** confident, clean, fast. Premium SaaS launch, light UI on white, not cartoonish. No voiceover, music + SFX only.

**Deliverables:** 30 s master in 16:9 (1920 × 1080, 60 fps), plus 9:16 and 1:1 cutdowns.
