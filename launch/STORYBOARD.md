**Scene 1 · Nobody's watching (hook)** — 0:00–0:04, frames 0–240
- VO: "Posting every day… and still nobody's watching?"
- On screen: a phone profile grid. Posts drop in one per day (MON → SUN), each with a views badge stuck at single digits. Day ticker counts up. Headline left: "Posting every day…" then "…and still *nobody's watching?*" (serif italic, orange).
- Motion: posts drop with overshoot, 3-frame stagger. View badges flicker 0–9. Headline word by word.
- Camera: slow push-in 100% → 106%.

**Scene 2 · Three gaps (problem)** — 0:04–0:08.6, frames 240–516
- VO: "Unedited footage. No personal brand. No strategy."
- On screen: three cards enter one per line, in sync with the VO: a raw clip thumbnail (`RAW_0042.MOV`, 14:32, shaky), a grey placeholder avatar ("@your_name — no bio"), a flat analytics line ("Reach −3%"). Each card gets a red ✕ stamp.
- Motion: cards drop in with overshoot on each VO line, ✕ stamps with a press.
- Camera: easy-ease pan left → right following the current card, slight push.

**Scene 3 · Meet I.S.P. (fix)** — 0:08.6–0:11.3, frames 516–678
- VO: "Meet I.S.P. Agency."
- On screen: problem cards are swept away (fast ease-in exit). Orange crescent draws around the ISP mark, "Agency" builds under it.
- Motion: crescent stroke draw, letters offset 3 frames, glow bloom.
- Camera: push-in 100% → 108%.

**Scene 4 · All in one team (fix)** — 0:11.3–0:16.8, frames 678–1008
- VO: "Video editing. Personal branding. Social media growth. All in one team."
- On screen: three service tiles, one per line: an editor timeline with clips and playhead; a brand profile card (orange avatar ring, verified tick, bio); a growth chart climbing. On "All in one team" the tiles slide together into one panel with the ISP mark and avatars of the team.
- Motion: tiles enter with overshoot, inner UI animates (playhead, ring draw, chart line draw). Merge with overlap.
- Camera: pan follows the active tile, then easy-ease pull back to frame the merged panel.

**Scene 5 · Raw → grows you (fix)** — 0:16.8–0:21.7, frames 1008–1302
- VO: "We turn your raw footage into content that actually grows you."
- On screen: editor UI. A raw clip on the timeline; cursor arcs in and clicks "Edit". Clip gets cut into segments, captions and colour grade appear on the preview, then it flies into a phone post. Views counter 0 → 128K and followers +12.4K.
- Motion: cursor on an arc with press, segments stagger, counter ease-out over 1 s, hold ≥ 1.5 s.
- Camera: zoom into the Edit button on the click (≈125%), ease back to 100% for the post.

**Scene 6 · You record (payoff)** — 0:21.7–0:24.5, frames 1302–1470
- VO: "You record. We handle the rest."
- On screen: left, a REC button pulses ("You record."). Right, a checklist ticks off with 3-frame offsets: Edit ✓ Captions ✓ Branding ✓ Posting ✓ Growth ✓ ("We handle the rest.").
- Motion: REC press, checkmarks draw staggered.
- Camera: gentle pan from REC to checklist.

**Scene 7 · Logo + CTA (payoff)** — 0:24.5–0:30, frames 1470–1800
- VO: "Book your free strategy call today. Link in bio."
- On screen: ISP logo, CTA button "Book your free strategy call" (cursor clicks it), "Link in bio ↓" and `@isp_agency_`.
- Motion: logo build, CTA with offset, button press, slow drift to the end, fade out the last 0.3 s.
- Camera: gentle push-in.
