# ISP Agency launch video: style prompt

This prompt rebuilds the ISP Agency launch video in the style of the Flowly reference (https://pin.it/24l05nprd), using the content and word timings of the ISP voiceover. The widget, notification and card wording is placeholder copy; edit it before use.

```text
Create a 36-second, 16:9 (1920×1080, 60 fps) launch video for ISP Agency, a creative agency that offers
video editing, personal branding, social media marketing, script writing and SaaS demo videos.
Use the supplied voiceover track as the master clock: every visual beat lands on the spoken word.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
OVERALL STYLE (copy exactly)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Look: dreamy, airy, premium Apple-style product film. Clean frosted-glass UI floating over bright,
  idyllic, saturated nature backdrops (soft depth of field, gentle motion in the footage).
  Never flat colour or gradient backgrounds, except the final dark end card.
• Backdrops by scene: white cherry blossoms against a clear blue sky → pink-and-gold sunset clouds →
  lush green hills under a blue sky with white clouds → a calm mountain lake with flowers in the
  foreground → a soft flower garden → sunny sky above the clouds → dark end card.
• UI surfaces:
  – Light frosted glass: white at 70–85% opacity, 18 px backdrop blur, 1.5 px white border,
    large radius (44 px cards, fully rounded pills), soft long shadow.
  – Dark glass panels: warm grey at 60–75% opacity, 30 px blur, 40 px radius, white text.
• Typography: Satoshi only, in two weights: Light (300) and Medium (500). Large, tight tracking
  (−0.03 to −0.05 em). Sentences mix the weights: lead-in words Light, key words Medium.
• Brand colour: ISP orange gradient #FFB866 → #FF7A1A → #DD4C06, used for highlights, buttons, pills and
  key words. Body text is near-black #141210 on light surfaces, white on dark.
• Cursor: classic white glove hand with a black outline. It moves on soft arcs, hesitates before
  clicks, and every click squashes the button (scale 1 → 0.94 → 1 over about 8 frames).
• Motion rules:
  – Nothing moves linearly: ease-in-out everywhere, slight overshoot on entrances.
  – The camera never stops: a constant slow push-in or drift.
  – Pace is a new beat every 1.5–3 s; never hold a still shot longer than about 3 s.
• Signature transitions (use these, in this order):
  1. Hard cut from an extreme close-up to the wide shot of the same UI.
  2. Pixel flash: a burst of white glowing squares, about 0.25 s.
  3. Circle iris: the outgoing shot shrinks into a circle and falls back while the next scene sits
     behind it; or a circle opens from a clicked button to reveal the next scene.
  4. Phone whip: the phone turns side-on toward camera and its dark edge wipes the frame.
  5. Lift-out: a highlighted line of text lifts out of its panel, becomes a pill, and the pill
     morphs into a button.
• Sound design (under the voiceover): deep sub-bass impact on every cut; short noise risers into
  transitions; a digital glitch hit on the pixel flash; crisp UI clicks; soft bubbly pops for each
  card or bubble; light key ticks while text types; a shimmer on the logo and the end card.
  Effects sit about 4 dB under the voice.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SCENES (times = voiceover timings)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1 · 0.0–2.1 s · CHERRY BLOSSOMS, extreme close-up
  A huge single-line frosted prompt pill with a "+" icon on the left fills the frame. The words type
  in exactly as spoken: "Video editing, personal branding,". The camera creeps in slowly.

2 · 2.1–4.3 s · same scene, WIDE (hard cut on "social")
  The full prompt card: "Video editing, personal branding, social media marketing & script writing",
  with "script writing" in Medium. There is a black "Generate" pill button bottom-right.
  The glove cursor arcs in, hesitates and clicks Generate at 4.0 s. The button shows a spinner.
  → pixel flash → circle iris shrinks the shot away.

3 · 4.3–5.9 s · PINK SUNSET CLOUDS · "All in one place?"
  A frosted-glass folder pops up (overshoot) with the ISP logo tile on its front. Four paper cards
  spring out of it one by one, on each word: Video editing · Personal branding · Social media ·
  Script writing. Then the folder shrinks away into the distance.

4 · 5.9–7.3 s · GREEN HILLS · "Meet ISP Agency."
  Kinetic type, centred. Each letter rises in on a wave: it starts below, rotated, blurred and
  orange, then settles into place. Final colours: "Meet" near-black Light, "ISP" orange Medium,
  "Agency." near-black Medium. The camera pans slowly to the right.

5 · 7.3–11.0 s · PINK SUNSET CLOUDS · "team of professional editors and experienced script writers"
  An iPhone (blue lock screen, 9:41) grows from tiny to full size in the centre.
  iOS-style widgets fly in from both sides with a slight tilt:
  – Notes-style cards: "Professional editors – 3+ years of experience", "First cut review 11:00".
  – A calendar widget: "Mon 24".
  – A script card: "Hook: 'Stop scrolling.'"
  – Chat bubbles: white "who's editing my reels?" and blue "need a script by friday".
  – A blue widget: "Meeting with your editor".
  Dark lock-screen notifications stack on the phone on the spoken words:
  "ISP Editors – your reel's first cut is ready" (on "editors"),
  "ISP Script Writers – 3 hooks drafted" (on "script writers").
  → phone whip transition.

6 · 11.0–16.0 s · MOUNTAIN LAKE · live transcript
  A dark glass panel grows in. Its header has a glowing orange dot and "ISP is listening".
  The transcript types in sync with the voice:
  "you: planning to edit your video,"
  "you: need a script, or want us to just handle everything?"
  "isp: yeah, we've got you." – this line gets an orange highlight sweep.
  Slow push-in on the panel throughout.

7 · 16.0–20.3 s · same lake · "worked with over 500 clients… delivered more than 1,000 videos"
  The highlighted line lifts out of the panel (the panel fades) and becomes an orange glass pill,
  "Yeah, we've got you.", which morphs into a wide orange button:
  "500+ clients | 1,000+ videos delivered". The numbers count up on the spoken numbers.
  The glove cursor clicks the button → a circle iris opens from it onto the hills.

8 · 20.3–22.0 s · GREEN HILLS · "So what do we actually do?"
  Same letter-wave kinetic type: "so what do we" near-black Light, "actually do?" orange Medium.
  The camera drifts right.

9 · 22.0–27.2 s · the backdrop blurs into a FLOWER GARDEN · services
  The headline shrinks to the top. Three numbered rows (01, 02, 03) appear on the spoken words.
  Each one is a solid glowing orange bar that sweeps in from the left, then resolves into dark glass
  with white text:
  "We edit your videos." · "We write your scripts." · "We even create demo videos for your SaaS product."
  The key verb in each row is orange.

10 · 27.2–33.5 s · SKY ABOVE THE CLOUDS · "Look, if you want to level up your content…"
  The rows merge into a frosted prompt card. It types on the words:
  "Level up my content, boost my sales & position my brand".
  The glove clicks Generate (spinner). An orange pill pops below: "You're in the right place."

11 · 33.5–36.0 s · END CARD · "Book a call with us today."
  A dark circle iris grows from the centre, holding a glowing "Book" (soft orange neon glow).
  It opens onto a near-black background with orange halftone dots glowing in from the bottom
  corners. "Book" dissolves into: the ISP logo tile (pops in with overshoot), then
  "Book a call with us today." ("Book a call" Medium, the rest Light), then "ispagency.co" below.

DELIVERY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
H.264 MP4, 1920×1080, 60 fps, voiceover plus sound effects mixed, peak −1 dBFS.
Keep all text at least 5% inside the frame edges.
```
