The brand in four answers

What is Ghost?

An open-source platform for publishing online and earning from it.

One product combines a website, an editor, email newsletters, paid memberships and analytics. Ghost describes itself as “a powerful app for professional publishers to create, share, and grow a business around their content.”

Who is it for?

People whose work is their writing and their audience.

  • Independent writers and newsletter creators
  • Journalists and local news outlets
  • YouTubers, podcasters, artists and other creators
  • Businesses doing content marketing
What problem does it solve?

Creators build audiences they don’t own.

  • Rented reach: social feeds decide who sees each post.
  • Platform fees: many newsletter platforms take a cut of every paid subscription.
  • Tool sprawl: a website, an email tool, payments and member lists all live in separate apps with separate bills.
Why Ghost?

It is built so the creator keeps control.

  • 0% payment fees. Ghost takes no cut of what readers pay.
  • You own your domain, your content and your member list.
  • Open source, independent, and funded 100% by its users (ghost.org).
  • $100M+ earned each year by publications on Ghost (ghost.org).
