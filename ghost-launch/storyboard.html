Ghost: own what you publish.

A 30-second launch video for Ghost, the open-source platform for independent publishers. It moves from attention to the problem, then to Ghost fixing it, then to the payoff, and ends on the logo and CTA. Music and SFX only, no voiceover.

Format16:9 · 1920×1080
Frame rate60 fps · H.264 mp4
Length30.0 s · 1800 frames
AudienceWriters, newsletter creators, indie media
AudioMusic + SFX, no VO
StackRemotion (React), UI as SVG
Brief
Company
Ghost is an open-source publishing platform that combines a website, newsletters, paid memberships and analytics, so independent creators don’t need a stack of separate tools.
Goal
Launch video for the website hero and social posts.
Message
Stop renting your audience. Own it with Ghost.
Must show
1. The hook: your post buried in someone else’s feed. 2. The problem: five tools and a fee on every subscription. 3. The product: write, then Publish & send in Ghost. 4. The payoff: members grow and you keep 100% of the revenue. 5. Logo + CTA.
Tone
Calm, confident and a little defiant. Premium SaaS launch, not cartoonish.

02 · PROBLEM
Scene 02 · Problem0:04–0:11 · f 240–660

The duct-taped stack

On screen
Five generic app windows: Website, Email, Payments, Members, Analytics. The cursor copies a subscriber row from Members, arcs over and pastes it into Email, then moves on to Payments. A revenue counter reads “$1,000”, a pink 10% slice cuts away, and the counter rolls down to “$900”. Text: “Five tools. Five bills. A cut of every sale.”
Motion
The windows drop in staggered, 3 frames apart, with overshoot, then jitter slightly. The cursor moves on bezier arcs, with click presses (scale 1 → 0.96 → 1 over 8 frames) and highlighted rows. The counter ticks down over 1 s and the slice falls out of frame with an ease-in. The text holds ≥ 1.5 s.
Camera
Easy-ease pan from left to right following the cursor (≥ 0.8 s), then a slight pull back to show all five windows.
Sound
Mouse clicks, a dull “cut” whoosh on the slice, and the pulse rising in pitch. Hard silence on the last frame.
Note
No competitor names or logos. verify The 10% figure is the common newsletter-platform fee and is shown as an illustration.

03 · FIX
Scene 03 · Fix0:11–0:19 · f 660–1140

Five tools collapse into one Ghost

On screen
The five windows from Scene 02 slide to the center and merge into the Ghost editor (SVG). The cursor types the title “The future of local news”, then clicks Publish & send. Four destination chips appear: Website, Newsletter, Members, Social web (Ghost 6). The post card flies into each one. Text: “One place. Ghost.”
Motion
The windows are parented under one null and animate as a group into the editor frame, with the editor entering during the final 20% of the merge. Typing runs one character per 2 frames. The button gets a press. The chips stagger in 3 frames apart with overshoot, and each lights up pink as the card arrives.
Camera
Zoom into the Publish & send button on the click (≈ 130%), then an easy-ease back to 100% to show the chips.
Sound
A warm major chord on the merge, when the main music track kicks in. Key clicks, then a “send” swoosh and four soft pings.
