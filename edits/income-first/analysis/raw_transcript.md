## Plain text

**Interviewer:** And what message would you give people who are watching this and who want to get into acting?

**Guest:** So my advice to anybody who wants to play this game is find something that brings you income first. If you can find a way to have those checks rolling in, you get into real estate, you get in the stock market, you find a full-time job, that brings the money in, then pursue this. Because if you are going to be what I call one of those starving actors, I mean, you're better off buying a lottery ticket.

**Interviewer:** Yeah.

**Guest:** So don't do that to yourselves. Don't do that to your families. I've seen actors make their families suffer, pursuing their dream, we'll call it. No, don't do that. This industry is not going to take care of you in that way.
