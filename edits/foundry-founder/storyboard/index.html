Shot by shot

Rules for every shot

Keeping the AI twin believable

  • No hard cuts between the twin and full-screen UI. The twin shrinks into a rounded glass card that lives in the same pale-cyan space as the app cards. The cut reads as a design move, so nobody compares his face against a crisp UI frame.
  • Cuts land only in the pauses between sentences, never mid-word, so lip-sync drift is never on screen at a cut point.
  • Each twin appearance runs 4 seconds or less, except the intro (6.8 s). He gets a slow 1.00 → 1.06 push so the frame never sits still.
  • Chest-up crop to keep hands out of frame. Light grain and a grade matched to the real test footage, which also softens the AI skin texture.
  • The real test recording is the reference for skin tone, framing and the white-door background. The twin is graded to match it.

Clip on every app scene

  • 150 px, bottom-right, 56 px from the edges, with a slow 2 px idle bob. It appears on every app and motion-graphic shot and hides on full-frame Eric shots.
  • Shot 12 is Clip's moment: it pops from the corner to centre stage, then goes back to its corner for the rest of the video.
  • It reacts on a few beats: a tap on "Clean Up", a nod when the payment lands. That's enough to feel alive without stealing attention.

Captions, burned in

  • Inter SemiBold, 44 px, white on a 74% navy frosted pill, centred 64 px from the bottom. Readable on both the light and dark scenes.
  • One line, 42 characters max. The keyword in each line is highlighted in Foundry teal (prices, payment methods, "theirs", "paid").
  • Captions are capped at 1180 px wide, so they never touch Clip in the corner.
  • Caption text follows the approved script spelling, e.g. "foundryconnects.com" and "Clip".

Music and mix

  • Licensed track (Artlist, Musicbed or Epidemic, license certificate included). Light, uplifting, 100–110 BPM, plucks and soft pads, no vocals.
  • Voice at −14 LUFS for web. Music sits around −28 LUFS under the voice with sidechain ducking, and rises to about −18 on the logo close.
  • Cuts and card pops land on the beat wherever the voiceover allows. Short UI ticks only on key beats: invoice sent, payment received.

What I still need from you

  • Clip PNGs with transparent backgrounds, plus any poses you have (waving, pointing, thumbs-up).
  • The first rough cut, so I can see what you didn't like.
  • The flyover in 1080p or 4K, or its project file. The upload is 1280×720, and upscaling softens the small UI text.
  • Logo as SVG, plus your brand hex codes and font.
  • Spanish versions of the app screens for the mirror-translation shot. Without them I'll rebuild the cards myself.
  • Screen recordings of client import, booking link and reviews, if they exist. Otherwise those become motion-graphic cards.
  • Approval to show Stripe, QuickBooks, Google and Facebook logos under their brand rules.
  • Is the flyover's own music licensed for reuse? If not, I'll replace it.
