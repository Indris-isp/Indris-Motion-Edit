# Applying the style to the Livesites scripts

The client doc is "Livesites Scripts". It has Ad Script #1 and #2, a VSL, a long vertical demo, and demo questions. Its structure is the same as the caption reel: **a hook with a result number → problem → "here's the fix" → demo proof → dashboard → offer → CTA.** So the caption reel's structure maps almost 1:1. The animation reel gives the motion for the brochure UI, the dashboard and the brand moments.

## Caption spec to use (copied from the reference)
- Inter Bold, lowercase, white, about 54 px on 1080×1920, tracking −2 %. Hard swaps, 1–3 words per chunk.
- **A-FULL:** no box, in the negative space by the face (y≈37 %; or on the chest by the hand in the hook).
- **SPLIT:** dark translucent pill on the seam.
- **B-WHITE:** light-grey pill under the graphic, following its bottom edge.
- **UI-DARK:** dark pill just above the UI's bottom bar.
- **CTA:** one word per chunk. Don't caption the keyword the lockup already shows.
- **Lockups** (big silver gradient title, with a kicker above and a sub-line below) **only** for: the hook number, the product name, the CTA.

## Motion spec to use (copied from the reference)
- Pick a music bed and **cut every section change on a kick**. Hide those cuts with a 1-frame white flash or a match-on-action.
- Text writes on with blur; it never pops. The camera always drifts. Motion blur and echo trails on fast moves.
- Dark UI world = "the AI thinking". A white world = "the result". Black + horizon glow = "brand".
- One accent colour (the Livesites brand colour, in place of Claude coral) + black / white / cream.
