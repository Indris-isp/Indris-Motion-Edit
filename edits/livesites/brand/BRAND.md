# Livesites brand (from Brand Guidelines v1.0, Oct 2026), as it applies to the edits

## Colour
| name | hex | use |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | #FBFBFA | Primary background. The B-WHITE canvas. |
| Mist | #F2F0F5 | Surfaces, chips, caption pills on light backgrounds |
| Ink | #1D1D1F | Text, the static layer |
| Night | #0E0C12 | Dark grounds. The "AI thinking" world and the end card. |
| Iris | #9A6BF0 | Gradient stop 1 |
| Orchid | #C07BFF | Gradient stop 2 (at 55 %) |
| Bloom | #F08BE0 | Gradient stop 3 |

- **Live gradient:** 135°, Iris → Orchid → Bloom. Use it **only** for live moments: the scan sweep, the send/ask button, answers, the mark. Target proportions: Paper 58 %, Mist 18 %, Ink 14 %, Night 6 %, gradient 4 %. "Orchid works like light, not paint."
- **Replacements for the reference colours:** Claude coral → the live gradient. The green scan sweep → a live-gradient scan. The orange rim-light → an Orchid rim-light.

## Type
- **Geist** for all language, and **Geist Mono** for labels, data and live states. For example: "LIVE · 2 VIEWERS · 14:02", Geist Mono 24, +2 %.
- Display: Geist Medium, 0.95 leading, −4.5 %. Headline: Geist Semibold, −3.5 %. Body: Geist Regular.
- The wordmark is "Livesites": one word, capital L. Never LiveSites, LIVESITES or livesites.
- **Captions:** Geist Semibold, lowercase, white. This replaces the reference's Inter Bold. Proper nouns keep their capitals ("Livesites", "UK").

## The star (brand device)
- A four-point star with concave sides. It means "something is live, listening or answering".
- One star per composition, and only where something is live. White on glass, Orchid on light. Never on Ink alone.

## Motion (brand rule, which wins over the reference where they conflict)
- "Calm, then alive. Layers move like glass, with weight and **no bounce**. The star is the only element allowed to sparkle, and only once."
- Logo animation:
  1. **Rest:** the ink square alone (0 ms).
  2. **Arrive:** the glass layer glides in from the top right (320 ms, ease-out).
  3. **Spark:** the star scales up with a slight overshoot, then rests (+400 ms).
- This replaces the Claude logo morph from the animation reference. We keep the reference's beat sync, flashes, write-on text and camera moves, but use ease-out without bounce.

## Logo
- Three marks: Primary (corner star), Compact (centre star) and Expressive (twinkle, hero and motion only, ≥120 px).
- Lockups: horizontal, stacked and symbol.
- On Night: a light static layer with full-colour glass. On the gradient: one-colour white, with the glass at 45 %.
- Don't recolour, rotate, stretch, swap the layers, make the glass opaque, add shadows or effects, use the star alone as the logo, or place it on busy colour.
