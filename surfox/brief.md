# SurFox AI — 30-second launch video

**Company:** SurFox AI is an AI lead responder for service businesses. When a lead texts, chats on the website or calls the published number, SurFox answers in seconds, qualifies them and books the call, 24/7. It takes speed-to-lead off the sales team's plate.

**Audience:** owners, sales and ops leads at small and mid-size service businesses (staffing, home services, agencies) that get inbound leads by text, web chat and phone.

**Goal:** launch video for paid social (Instagram first), the website hero and Product Hunt.

**One message:** cold leads become calendar holds, without the chase.

**Must show:**
1. The hook: a lead texts at 11:52 PM, and nobody's awake.
2. The problem: the business sees it the next morning (9h 22m later), and the lead has already booked someone who answered.
3. The product: SurFox answering on SMS, website chat and voice, and running a real qualifying conversation (urgency, shift, pay) in the chat UI.
4. The payoff: "Hot · qualified" lead, then "Call booked 4:00 PM, handed off to your team". Runs 24/7. Plans from $49/mo.
5. Logo + CTA: "Try it free in minutes", getsurfox.com.

**Tone:** confident, clean, fast. Premium SaaS launch, not cartoonish. Dark navy night scenes against bright white product scenes, with teal as the brand accent. No voiceover, music + SFX only.

**Deliverables:** 30 s master in 16:9 (1920 × 1080, 60 fps), plus a 9:16 cutdown (1080 × 1920) for Reels and Stories.

**Assets:** UI rebuilt as vectors from the site's hero (chat, lead card, booking card). Brand: teal `#0F7C86` / `#16A6AE`, glow `#3FE0E8`, navy `#0B1220`; Inter + Instrument Serif Italic. The fox mark is a placeholder vector, to be swapped for the official logo SVG.

**Styleframes:** `surfox/stills/01.png` to `12.png`, the approved look for every scene.
